# Topic: verify real-world car fault diagnosis facts (for a kids' repair game's fault catalog), each with a source link

Verify or correct each claim, give one reliable link per item (manufacturer, AAA, NHTSA, Bosch/NAPA/AutoZone/RepairPal/Haynes guides, SAE code lists):
1. 12 V lead-acid battery resting voltage: ~12.6 V full, ~12.2 V half, <=11.9 V discharged; engine running charging voltage normal ~13.7-14.7 V.
2. Clicking no-start causes: weak battery, corroded/loose terminals, bad starter/solenoid; white/green crust on terminals = corrosion, clean with baking soda + wire brush.
3. OBD-II codes meanings: P0562 system voltage low; P0217 engine overheat; P0128 coolant thermostat below regulating temp; P0300-P0304 misfire (random / cylinder 1-4); swapping an ignition coil to another cylinder and seeing whether the misfire code moves = classic test.
4. Tires: US minimum tread depth 2/32 inch, wear bars, penny test; inner-edge wear = alignment (camber/toe); center wear = overinflation; both edges = underinflation; cupping = worn shocks/struts; sidewall bulge = must replace (no patch); punctures repairable only in tread area (plug-patch), not sidewall/shoulder (USTMA guidance).
5. Steering wheel shake at highway speed = wheel imbalance (missing weight) common cause.
6. Brake pads: replace around 3 mm (or 2-3 mm) friction; squeal from wear indicator tab; grinding = metal backing on rotor; pulsating pedal = rotor thickness variation ("warped"); replace pads per axle in pairs.
7. Coolant leak: sweet smell, green/orange/pink puddle at front; steam from hood; cooling fan not running = overheating in traffic but OK on highway.
8. Exhaust: loud roar = hole in muffler/pipe; rattling = broken rubber hanger or loose heat shield.
9. Smoke colours: white (coolant / head gasket), blue (burning oil), black (rich fuel mixture, e.g. clogged air filter or sensor).
10. Struts: bounce test (more than ~1-2 oscillations = worn), oil leaking on strut body, clunk over bumps.
11. Alternator failure: battery warning light, dimming lights, whining noise, new battery keeps dying.
12. Serpentine belt squeal on cold start = worn/loose belt; cracks.
Answer in Korean, concise table: claim | verdict (맞음/고침) | corrected value | link.
